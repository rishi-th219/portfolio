Engineering perspective

“Data without a story is just noise — the real value is built where fragmented operational records meet dimensional clarity.”

Across statewide public healthcare institutions at CHiPS, millions of biometric punches lived completely apart from State Treasury payroll ledgers. By combining deterministic SQL CTE identity resolution with a clean Kimball Star Schema, scattered operational telemetry became an authoritative single source of truth.

Alongside production SQL and Power BI systems, I am actively bridging into cloud data engineering — preparing for AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner and AWS Data Engineer Associate (DEA-C01) to scale dimensional marts across S3, Athena, Glue, and Redshift.
