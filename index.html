Background

About Me & The Transition to Engineering

My work in data started by answering operational questions: Where are the attendance bottlenecks? Which facilities have reporting gaps? What do the staffing trends predict?

As a Data Analyst Intern at CHiPS (Chhattisgarh Infotech Promotion Society), posted under the Secretary's office of the Department of Health & Family Welfare, I analyzed operational metrics across 33 districts and 1,000+ public healthcare facilities.

Working with raw data across disparate databases—biometrics (AEBAS), state treasury payroll, hospital OPD systems, and drug procurement—taught me that dashboards are only as reliable as the underlying data architecture.

When source systems lack shared keys and require multi-attribute fuzzy matching to reconcile records, ad-hoc queries break down. That insight pushed me to dive deep into Kimball dimensional modeling, automated ELT pipelines with dbt, containerized Airflow DAG orchestration, and database optimization.

Analytical Foundation

KPI formulation, DAX measures, requirements translation, top-down drill-through hierarchies (District → Facility → Designation → Employee), and isolating actionable trends.

Engineering Mindset

Modular dbt staging and mart layers, Kimball Star Schema modeling, schema testing assertions, SCD Type 2 tracking, and containerized Docker environments.

Target Roles

Data Analyst, Analytics Engineer, and Junior Data Engineer opportunities where I can apply strong analytical rigor and continue scaling data pipelines.
