Background & Trajectory

About Me & The Transition to Data Engineering

My journey in data began with an ambition to answer fundamental operational questions: Where are the bottlenecks? Which resources are under-utilized? What do the trends predict?

As a Data Analyst Intern at CHiPS (Chhattisgarh Infotech Promotion Society), posted under the Secretary's office of the Department of Health & Family Welfare, I was given real-world responsibility. I analyzed attendance and operational metrics across 33 districts and 1,000+ public healthcare facilities.

Working with raw data across multiple distinct databases—such as biometrics (AEBAS), state treasury payroll, hospital OPD systems, and drug procurement warehouses—taught me a vital lesson: dashboards are only as dependable as the underlying data architecture.

When source systems lack shared primary keys and require multi-attribute fuzzy matching to reconcile employee records, simple ad-hoc queries fall short. That realization motivated me to dive deep into Kimball dimensional modeling, automated ELT pipelines with dbt, containerized Airflow DAG orchestration, and database optimization.

📊 The Analytical Foundation

Strong focus on KPI formulation, DAX calculations, stakeholder requirements translation, top-down drill-through hierarchies (District → Facility → Employee), and finding actionable signals in complex datasets.

⚡ The Engineering Mindset

Building reliable pipelines: modular dbt staging and mart layers, Kimball Star Schema modeling, automated testing assertions, SCD Type 2 tracking, and containerized Docker environments.

🎯 Target Roles

Data Analyst, Business Analyst (Operations/WHS), Analytics Engineer, and Junior Data Engineer opportunities where I can apply strong analytical rigor and continue scaling data pipelines.

The work at the Health Department was structured as an incremental, expanding data foundation—each phase built directly upon the pipeline, data reconciliation, and schemas established in the previous phase:

Phase 1 • DHS & AEBAS Data

Statewide Attendance Intelligence

Engineered a 6-page interactive Power BI reporting suite on MariaDB covering all employees statewide across 33 districts. Built a top-down drilldown architecture: District → Facility → Designation → Employee. Resolved severe data quality issues including inconsistent names, duplicate designations, and separated 3 distinct employment tiers (regular, contractual, and NHM).

Phase 2 • Biometrics + State Treasury

Bonded Doctors Tracking & Identity Resolution

Mandatory service doctors (MBBS/MD) were posted across 6 facility levels (PHC to Medical Colleges). Many failed to report while drawing pay. Because AEBAS attendance and Treasury payroll had no shared key, I engineered a fuzzy-matching ETL layer mapping attendance IDs to treasury codes and categorized staff into 3 actionable defaulter buckets.

Phase 3 • NIC Next-Gen e-Hospital

End-to-End Doctor Performance Pipeline

Linked biometric attendance and treasury incentive payouts with outpatient (OPD) consultation volumes from the Government of India's next-gen e-Hospital platform. Reconciled disparate backend architectures developed independently by different National Informatics Centre (NIC) teams into a single unified performance dashboard.

Phase 4 • CGMSCL Logistics (Internal Work)

Internal Drug Procurement & ABC/VED Analytics

Conducted internal supply chain and procurement analytics for the Chhattisgarh Medical Services Corporation Ltd (CGMSCL) across EDL and Non-EDL drug categories. Implemented multi-criteria ABC (spend volume), VED (clinical criticality: Vital, Essential, Desirable), and SDE (lead-time difficulty) analysis in Oracle DB to identify dead stock and support critical reorder planning in Oracle Analytics Cloud.
